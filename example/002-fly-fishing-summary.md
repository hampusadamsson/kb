Fly fishing is an angling technique using an ultra-lightweight artificial fly lure that mimics insects to catch insectivorous fish. Because flies are too light to cast conventionally, it uses specialized tackle with heavy fly lines and distinct casting techniques. Anglers often wear waders, carry a hand net, and stand in the water. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fly_fishing
