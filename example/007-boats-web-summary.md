A boat is a watercraft of many types and sizes, generally smaller than a ship (distinguished by size/capacity, shape, or ability to be carried aboard another vessel). Small boats are typically used on inland waterways such as rivers and lakes, or in protected coastal areas; some (e.g. whaleboats) were designed for offshore use. In modern naval terms, a boat is a vessel small enough to be carried aboard a ship; submarines are also referred to as boats.

All boats share a hull, whose form (e.g. planing vs displacement) determines handling and performance for the intended use. Common categories include powerboats, sailboats, fishing boats, rowing/rowing-type craft, and work vessels — each tailored to a purpose such as leisure cruising, sport/fishing, or commercial work.
