A train is a series of connected vehicles running along a railway track, transporting people or freight. Usually pulled or pushed by locomotives, though some are self-propelled (multiple units, railcars). Carried in railroad cars (wagons/carriages). Built to a set gauge; steel wheels on steel track give low friction and high efficiency. Source: Wikipedia.
